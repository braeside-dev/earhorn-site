If EarHorn is installed on this phone, the link you tapped should have opened it directly. If you are reading this instead, the app is not on this phone yet, or your phone has decided to open EarHorn links in Safari.

Get EarHorn

EarHorn is coming to the App Store. Until then, the person who shared this link can send you an invitation to the test version.

About EarHorn

After installing

Scan the code on the nursery phone again with your phone's Camera app, or ask for the link to be sent again. A pairing link works for two hours and cannot be carried through an install, so a fresh one is needed once the app is on your phone.

If the link keeps opening in Safari

Your phone remembers a choice to open EarHorn links here instead of in the app. Touch and hold the link where it was sent to you, then choose Open in EarHorn. Or open EarHorn, choose the parent role, tap Enter a code instead, and type the code shown under the QR on the nursery phone.

What this link is

It carries the nursery phone's address on your home network and a one-time secret, and it lets a phone pair with that nursery for two hours. Treat it like a key: do not forward it, and do not post it anywhere.
